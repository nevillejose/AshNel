Privacy Policy & Data Charter

ASHNEL INC. acts as an institutional Data Fiduciary committed to sovereign data protection, purpose-limited processing, and zero tracking under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India). We do not monetize, broker, or surveil client communications.

Statutory Framework: DPDP Act, 2023 • Data Fiduciary: ASHNEL INC. (Bangalore) • Effective: September 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: Zero Tracking & Sovereign Ownership

Unlike marketing-driven agencies, ASHNEL INC. operates purely as a B2B software engineering and digital product studio. We employ no third-party behavioral trackers, no Meta/TikTok/LinkedIn surveillance pixels, and no automated profiling algorithms. Any personal data collected is provided voluntarily through affirmative consent solely to scope and deliver bespoke engineering services.

SECTION 02

Categories of Personal Data Collected

In adherence to the statutory principle of Data Minimization (Section 6(1), DPDP Act), ASHNEL INC. collects only the strict quantum of information necessary to formulate engineering estimates and execute contractual milestones:

Scope & Estimator Inquiries

When you submit a project scope through our Interactive Estimator or Contact Desk: your Name, Work Email Address, Phone Number / WhatsApp (optional), Corporate Entity Name, Target Timeline, Budget Tier, and Functional Architectural Requirements.

Technical & Contractual Execution

Upon formal engagement under a Statement of Work: Authorized Representative credentials, billing/GSTIN coordinates, staging server environment access keys (transferred via encrypted zero-knowledge vaults), and authorized team contact lists.

Server Telemetry & Logs

Standard HTTP request headers (IP address, user-agent string, timestamp, requested URI) collected automatically at our Nginx edge server strictly for network diagnostics, DDoS prevention, and rate-limiting. These logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days and never joined with personal identities.

Zero Prohibited Data

We do not collect children's personal data (Section 9 DPDP Act), biometric data, health identifiers, financial account numbers, or behavioral profiling telemetry.

SECTION 03

Lawful Grounds & Purpose Limitation

Under Sections 4, 6, and 7 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data is processed solely under the following two lawful grounds:

1. Explicit Affirmative Consent (Section 6)

Obtained via an affirmative, non-pre-checked action prior to submitting the Scope Estimator or Contact Desk. You have the right to withdraw this consent at any time by emailing privacy@ashnel.com.

2. Certain Legitimate Uses (Section 7)

Processing required for the performance of a contractual Statement of Work (SOW), issuance of statutory GST tax invoices under Indian law, compliance with judicial subpoenas, and prevention of fraudulent or cyber attacks on our production nodes.

Strict Purpose Limitation Covenant: We will never monetize, lease, or distribute your contact information to marketing aggregators, third-party software brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances.

SECTION 04

Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Principal whose digital personal data is processed by ASHNEL INC., you possess guaranteed rights under Chapter III of the DPDP Act, 2023:

Right to Access Information (Section 11) Guaranteed

You are entitled to receive a structured summary of your personal data being processed, the specific purposes of processing, and the identities of any authorized service providers who have handled it.

Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12) Guaranteed

You may request correction of inaccurate or misleading information, completion of incomplete data, or complete erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the initial scoping or legal purpose.

Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13) Guaranteed

You have an immediate, readily accessible institutional mechanism to register grievances with our Data Protection Officer. If unresolved within 30 days, you retain the statutory right to escalate your complaint to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).

Right to Nominate (Section 14) Guaranteed

You have the statutory right to nominate any other individual who shall, in the event of your death or incapacity, exercise your Data Principal rights on your behalf.

To exercise any statutory right, submit a signed electronic request to our Grievance Desk: privacy@ashnel.com
SECTION 05

Data Retention & Storage Limitation

Under Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act, personal data is permanently expunged or anonymized once the specified purpose has been realized or upon withdrawal of consent:

  • Unengaged Scoping Inquiries: Purged from active communication logs within 180 calendar days of receipt.
  • Contractual Engineering Client Data: Kept active for the duration of the build sprint plus the 45-day warranty window, after which credentials and architectural sandboxes are wiped or surrendered to client custody.
  • Statutory Invoices & Billing Records: Retained for 7 fiscal years to satisfy mandatory Indian Goods & Services Tax (GST) and Income Tax audit regulations.
SECTION 06

Security Safeguards & Cross-Border Transfers

In compliance with Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act, ASHNEL INC. implements institutional, administrative, and technical safeguards to prevent personal data breaches:

  • Transport Security: All client interactions and estimator submissions are secured via TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Zero Surveillance Architecture: Zero third-party pixel injection, zero CDN telemetry harvesting, and zero invasive fingerprinting scripts on any public web property.
  • Cross-Border Transfer Compliance: All personal data is processed in accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act, 2023. We do not transfer personal data to countries restricted by the Central Government of India.
  • Breach Notification: In the unlikely event of a personal data breach, ASHNEL INC. will intimate the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals in the form and manner prescribed under the Act.
