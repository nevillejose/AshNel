Contractual Governance & IP Charter

Terms of Engagement

These Terms of Engagement govern all consulting, custom software engineering, cloud architecture advisory, and turnkey platform deployments executed by ASHNEL INC. We operate on fixed-scope Statements of Work (SOW), absolute code ownership transfer, and verified sovereign data stewardship.

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version: 3.4 (Institutional Edition) • Governing Law: Bangalore, India
Section 01

1. Fixed-Scope Statements of Work (SOW)

1.1 Milestone-Based Governance: All engineering projects undertaken by ASHNEL INC. are executed exclusively under written Statements of Work (SOW) detailing verified deliverables, architectural blueprints, completion criteria, and milestone fee schedules.

1.2 No Hourly Billable Traps: We explicitly repudiate open-ended Time & Material (T&M) billing models that incentivize agency bloat or delayed project completions. The total fee specified in an executed SOW represents the complete and capped cost for the agreed scope.

1.3 Scope Variation & Change Orders: Any functional alterations or expansion requested by the Client outside the agreed SOW boundaries are documented via written Change Requests with predetermined milestone adjustments before commencement.

Section 02

2. Intellectual Property & 100% Code Handover

2.1 Complete Client Ownership: Upon successful settlement of agreed milestone compensation, 100% of bespoke source code, database architectures, user interface templates, APIs, and custom configuration scripts developed specifically for the Client under the applicable SOW transfer to the Client.

2.2 Zero Vendor Lock-In: All repositories, cryptographic credentials, deployment keys, and documentation are handed over in standard formats. The Client retains uninhibited authority to host, modify, scale, or audit the software independently or with third-party engineering teams.

2.3 Open-Source Frameworks & Libraries: Software developed may utilize standard open-source technologies (e.g., Django, PostgreSQL, Frappe, ERPNext, Tailwind CSS, Redis). Each open-source component remains governed by its respective permissive license (MIT, BSD, Apache 2.0, GPL), with zero proprietary licensing encumbrances imposed by ASHNEL INC.

Section 03

3. Data Sanctity & Zero Commercialization Covenant

3.1 Sovereign Digital Enclaves: ASHNEL INC. engineers systems designed to operate within the Client’s sovereign infrastructure. We maintain zero backdoors, administrative master keys, or undisclosed telemetry endpoints in production systems.

3.2 Strict Non-Commercialization: ASHNEL INC. will never sell, lease, scrape, aggregate, or train public AI models on Client organizational, ecclesiastical, customer, or member records.

3.3 Surveillance Tracker Prohibition: In alignment with our published treatise The All-Seeing Digital Eyes, we do not embed third-party programmatic tracking pixels, surveillance brokers, or ad monetization beacons in software architected for community or institutional clients.

Section 04

4. Invoicing, Payments & MSME Statutory Compliance

4.1 MSME Registration & GST: ASHNEL INC. is a registered enterprise under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, Government of India. All corporate engagements are invoiced in full compliance with Goods and Services Tax statutes under Karnataka GSTIN: 29BPUPJ7485N1ZK.

4.2 Milestone Invoicing: Invoices are generated against verified milestones (e.g., Sprint 1 Kickoff, Alpha Architecture Demo, Production Deployment). Invoices are due within fifteen (15) calendar days of issuance unless otherwise stipulated in the SOW.

4.3 Statutory Interest: Payments delayed beyond contractual terms are governed by the statutory interest provisions of Chapter V of the MSMED Act, 2006.

Section 05

5. Engineering Warranties & Defect Remediation

5.1 Production Warranty Period: ASHNEL INC. provides an unconditional forty-five (45) day defect remediation warranty commencing upon production deployment. Any deviations, critical software bugs, or functional errors contradicting the SOW will be resolved expeditiously at zero incremental charge.

5.2 Exclusions: The warranty does not cover issues resulting from unauthorized third-party modifications, upstream cloud outage events, or deliberate Client misconfigurations.

Section 06

6. Confidentiality & Mutual Non-Disclosure

6.1 Confidential Information: Both parties covenant to treat all business records, technical blueprints, architecture designs, financial details, and client member data disclosed during the engagement as strictly confidential.

6.2 Non-Disclosure Term: Confidentiality covenants survive termination of the commercial engagement for a minimum period of five (5) years, with member data and trade secrets protected in perpetuity.

Section 07

7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Liability Cap: To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the aggregate liability of either party arising out of or related to an engagement under an SOW shall not exceed the total fees actually received by ASHNEL INC. under that specific SOW in the preceding six (6) months.

7.2 Consequential Damages: Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including lost profits or business disruption.

Section 08

8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

8.1 Jurisdiction: These Terms and any associated SOW shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the Republic of India.

8.2 Dispute Resolution: In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim, the parties shall first endeavor to resolve the matter amicably through direct senior executive consultation. Unresolved disputes shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Bangalore, Karnataka, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8.3 Court Jurisdiction: Subject to arbitration, the courts located in Bangalore, Karnataka, shall exercise exclusive jurisdiction.
